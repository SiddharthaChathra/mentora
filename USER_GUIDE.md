# USER_GUIDE.md

## Everyone
- Sign in with the email you were invited with. Press `/` or `Ctrl K` to search, `?` for shortcuts. "Show me around" in the avatar menu replays the tour.
- The semester switcher in the header focuses dashboards on one semester.
- Notifications: Mark done, Open, or Snooze 1 day. Every action has Undo.
- Account: photo, password, signed-in devices, email preferences, low-bandwidth mode.

## Students
1. New here? Use **Create an account** on the sign-in page (students only) with your **USN email** (for example `nnm23cs001@…`). Your branch is read from the USN (`cs` → CSE) and can’t be changed by you. Then finish the setup wizard (it saves as you go).
2. Academic records → Add semester. On a computer, type or paste marks into the grid (Tab moves right, Enter moves down); subjects for the semester are filled in from the catalogue. On a phone, enter one subject at a time.
3. Submit the semester when it matches your official result. Your mentor verifies it. After that, ask for a correction if something is wrong.
4. Feedback & plan: read notes, tick goal steps, add reflections. Resources and Sessions show what your mentor shared.
5. Official PDF reports (verified records only) are issued by your mentor or HOD — ask them for a copy.

## Mentors
- Overview: average SGPA, who needs attention (with reasons), follow-ups, sessions, subject performance (select a bar to see who took it).
- My mentees: filters, saved views, open a profile to recommend, upload or schedule.
- Verifications: check marks, verify in bulk or return with a note.
- Reports: preview or download a student's PDF.

## HOD (your branch)
- Everything you see is your own branch; the header chip shows it (e.g. CSE). Invite students and mentors into your branch; HODs are appointed by the Principal.
- Student profile → **Request branch transfer** sends a request to the Principal.
- Grading and risk rules follow the college default unless the Principal allows branch-specific grading.
- If your appointment ends you’ll see “Your HOD access has ended” on your next page load.
- Overview: coverage, workload, risk overview, section SGPA (select a section to drill down), next step.
- Assignments: drag students between mentors; changes can be undone and are audited.
- Users & roles, Settings (grading scheme, risk thresholds, sections, subjects), Imports/Exports, Audit log, Interventions.

## Principal (whole college)
- **College** overview: mentoring coverage, branches, students, unassigned, flagged and pending transfers; branch comparison (SGPA, coverage, missing records); branch health table; "Needs your attention".
- **Branch switcher** in the header (a sheet on phones) narrows every page to one branch; "All branches" brings everything back. It's remembered.
- **Students**: grouped by branch (or one table), read-only profiles. You can download PDF reports (audited), but never see private mentor notes and can't edit records, verify or assign mentors.
- **Branches**: create, rename, reorder, archive (only when empty). **HODs**: appoint, change or end an appointment (history kept). **Transfers**: approve/reject HOD requests or move a student directly. Their mentor assignment ends and their records move with them.
- **Users**, **Imports** (choose the branch first), **Exports** (college or one branch), **Settings** (college grading scheme, risk rules, academic years, branch-specific grading switch, sign-up rules), **Audit history** (everything).
